Investment Tips

💡 Start Early
The earlier you invest, the more time compound interest has to grow your wealth. Even small amounts make a big difference over time.
📅 Invest Consistently
Regular monthly contributions — even small ones — can dramatically increase your final returns through dollar-cost averaging.
🔄 Reinvest Your Returns
Compounding works best when you reinvest your earnings. Choose monthly or quarterly compounding for maximum growth.

Understanding Interest

🔄 Compound Interest
Interest is calculated on both the principal and previously earned interest. The more frequently it compounds, the more you earn.
📊 Simple Interest
Interest is calculated only on the principal amount. It grows linearly over time and is easier to predict.
